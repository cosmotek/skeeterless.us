Why CO2 Traps

Mosquitoes hunt by CO2. So that's exactly what we give them — away from you.

Mosquitoes track down a blood meal primarily by following a trail of exhaled CO2. Our traps mimic that signal, releasing a steady stream of CO2 alongside a proven attractant to draw mosquitoes toward the trap and intercept them before they ever reach your patio, deck, or garden.

There are no pesticides and no toxins anywhere in this system. Traditional spraying kills pollinators like bees and butterflies along with everything else it touches, and puts chemicals into the air your family breathes. Our traps emit only CO2 and bait, contained inside the unit — nothing drifts onto your lawn, your garden beds, or into your yard.

  • 100% chemical-free. No pesticides, no toxins, no spraying — ever. The system only emits CO2 and bait inside the trap itself.
  • Safe for kids, pets & pollinators. Bees, butterflies, and other beneficial insects go about their business undisturbed — nothing toxic enters their environment.
  • Works around the clock. Continuous trapping, not a one-time treatment that fades after a few days.
  • Military-grade technology. The same trap-and-lure principle relied on in U.S. Army and municipal mosquito control programs.
  • Discreet by design. Installed at the edge of your property — out of the way, out of sight.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this safe for kids, pets, and pollinators?

Yes. The system is 100% chemical-free — no pesticides, no toxins, no spraying anywhere on your property. It relies solely on CO2 and an attractant contained within the trap, so bees, butterflies, and other pollinators are never exposed to anything harmful.

Does SkeeterLess spray pesticides?

No — never. Traditional spraying kills pollinators indiscriminately and puts chemicals into the air around your home. Our traps don't spray anything; they only emit CO2 and bait inside a contained unit to lure mosquitoes away from your family.

How long until I notice fewer mosquitoes?

Most properties see a noticeable reduction within the first few weeks as the trap works through the local mosquito population.

Do I need to do anything after install?

No. Our monthly maintenance visits handle CO2 refills, bait replacement, and inspection — you don't manage the system yourself.

Can this work on any property?

Nearly any layout can be accommodated. Pricing reflects how complex the install is — service area and how CO2 lines route through your landscape are the biggest factors.

What if I need more than one trap?

Multi-trap installs are available for larger residential or commercial properties. Ask for a custom quote and we'll design a layout for your space.
